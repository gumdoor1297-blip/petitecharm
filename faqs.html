Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Petite Charm jewelry

Every piece at Petite Charm is specifically designed for women 5'4" and under. We've carefully calculated chain lengths, pendant sizes, ring dimensions, and bracelet circumferences to perfectly complement petite frames. Regular jewelry often overwhelms smaller frames or requires constant adjustments - our pieces are made to fit you perfectly from the start! We understand that proportions matter, and a necklace that looks great on someone 5'8" might hang too low on someone 5'2". That's why every measurement is intentional.
We use only premium materials including 14k gold, sterling silver, rose gold plating, genuine freshwater pearls, Swarovski crystals, and ethically sourced gemstones. All our pieces are nickel-free and hypoallergenic, perfect for sensitive skin. We believe in quality that lasts, which is why we use 14k gold filled (not plated) for many pieces - it's more durable and won't tarnish. Our sterling silver is always 925 grade, and our diamonds come with certificates of authenticity.
We offer rings in sizes 4-7, which are the most common sizes for petite women. If you're unsure of your size, you can use our printable ring sizer (available on the product page) or visit a local jewelry store for professional sizing. You can also measure an existing ring that fits well using our online guide. We're also happy to help with exchanges if you need a different size - just contact us within 30 days!
We offer FREE standard shipping (5-7 business days) on all orders over $50. Orders under $50 have a flat shipping rate of $5.99. Express shipping (2-3 days) is available for $14.99, and overnight shipping for $24.99. International shipping is available to most countries with delivery times of 7-14 business days. All orders are processed within 1-2 business days and come with tracking information. We ship Monday through Friday, excluding holidays.
Yes! We offer a 30-day return policy for unworn, undamaged items in their original packaging. Returns are free and easy - just contact us at returns@petitecharm.com for a prepaid return label. To keep your Petite Charm jewelry looking beautiful: Remove jewelry before showering, swimming, or exercising. Store pieces separately in a cool, dry place (we include a complimentary jewelry pouch with each order). Clean gently with a soft cloth - avoid harsh chemicals. Apply perfume and lotion before putting on jewelry. For sterling silver, use a silver polishing cloth occasionally to maintain shine. With proper care, your pieces will last for years!
Many of our pieces feature adjustable elements! Our necklaces often have 2" extension chains (for example, 14-16"), bracelets are adjustable within a 1" range, and anklets can be adjusted to fit comfortably. This ensures the perfect fit for your petite frame. Product descriptions always specify if an item is adjustable. Even our non-adjustable pieces are designed with petite proportions in mind.
